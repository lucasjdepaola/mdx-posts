Acquiring datatypes.com was initially used with the intention of developing a fully fledged GUI type-system standard, with a query language
included called equery. However, as the project became more ambitious and abstract, the scope and intention of the project became misguided.
A new iteration of datatypes will eventually occur, but for now, the domain sits.
